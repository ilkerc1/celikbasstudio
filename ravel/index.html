The daily collection

Six ways to untangle
an idea.

One puzzle of each type, every day. Familiar rules, different challenges, and a new set waiting tomorrow.

Mines

Read the numbers to reveal safe cells and flag the mines. Each board can be solved using logic, without guessing.

Numberlink

Connect matching numbers with paths that never cross. Complete every pair and fill every cell.

Binairo

Fill the grid with two symbols. Keep every row and column balanced, with no three identical symbols in a row.

Sudoku

Fill the classic 9×9 grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 exactly once.

Nonogram

Use the row and column clues to decide which cells to fill, revealing the picture hidden in the grid.

Star Battle

Place one star in every row, column and region. No two stars may touch, even diagonally.

Your daily thread

One solved puzzle
keeps it going.

Finish at least one puzzle each day to continue your streak. Solve all six for a perfect day, then share a result that leaves the answers to everyone else.

A new set arrives at local midnight. Players on the same calendar date get the same puzzles, so there is always a shared challenge to talk about.
